In a trading session the chain is sorted by moneyness, not by strike. ATM is the balanced bet, OTM is cheap and needs a move, DEEP ITM behaves almost like the stock. Pick a call or a put, then B to buy, S to write, Tab to change expiry, 1–5 to pick a rung (Shift for the put).

Contracts outlive the day. 0DTE dies at tonight's bell, weeklies run to Friday's close, and a LEAP lives a quarter — but you only get one slot. Anything still open is in the BOOK tab.

Walking is free. Energy is spent on actions — trading, classes, the gym, working a room. The gym raises your stamina ceiling permanently; the corner store sells it back to you by the can, at a steeply rising price.

The market runs 9:30 to 4:00 on weekdays and a session lasts until the bell, so get there early. Rent is due every seven days. Sleep at home to start the next day.
