# Heatseeker

## Heatseeker — Broker

A top-down 3D life sim about a stock broker's career, in `game/`.

Start at twenty-four in your parents' basement with $240. Walk the city, take a
job on a brokerage floor, learn to read a tape, and climb — then choose whether
you become **the Legend** (the best trader alive), **the House** (own the floor
everyone else works on), or **the Villain** (run the fund the whole city hates).

Open `game/index.html` in a browser. No build step and no network needed —
three.js is vendored locally and every texture is generated at load.

See [game/README.md](game/README.md) for controls and how the systems work.

---

`index.html` at the root is the earlier Heatseeker Autopilot trading dashboard,
unrelated to the game.
